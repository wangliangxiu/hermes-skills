# Train-Side POV Transition Technique (横版列车穿越转场)

## Overview

A horizontally-oriented narrative technique for railway/transportation timeline stories. The viewer stands beside the railway track (low angle, eye-level) and watches a train pass from right to left. Each carriage represents a different era. The transition between eras is a **pass-through** moment: the camera moves through a door or window frame into the next era.

## Fixed camera rules

- **Perspective:** low angle, eye-level, perpendicular to train direction
- **NOT aerial/overhead** — viewer is standing on the ground beside the track
- The view composition is always: train body crossing the frame horizontally + city/town silhouette in the background
- Train direction: right-to-left = past-to-present

## Three-act symmetrical structure

### Act 1 — 1902 (steam era, coal freight)

```
[Steam locomotive] → [Coal wagon① full] → [Coal wagon② half] → [Coal wagon③ empty with open doors]
```

- Steam locomotives had no passenger windows (correct period detail)
- Coal wagons have side-doors that open wide
- Coal: first wagon full (mounded), second half-full, third empty with doors open
- Sunlight shines through the empty wagon, illuminating coal-dust residue on the floor

**Transition:** Camera passes through the empty wagon's open side-door. The door frame fills the screen momentarily, then opens into Act 2.

### Act 2 — 1970 (green train era)

```
Looking at: a green train (绿皮车) below the camera position
Background: old city skyline — brick buildings, factory chimneys
```

- Camera emerges from the door-pass-through at the same low angle
- A green passenger train is visible below/on the tracks
- Background shows the era's city — not specific buildings, just general atmosphere (red brick buildings, chimneys, hazy sky)

**Zoom in:** Camera slowly zooms toward one window of the green train.

**Girl at window:** A young girl with pigtails, wearing floral-print clothes, chin resting on crossed arms on the window sill, looking outward curiously. The window frame is green painted metal.

**Focus shift:** While still zooming in, focus shifts from the girl to **another window behind her** (further along the train).

**Transition:** Camera passes through that second window. The window frame fills the screen → opens into Act 3.

### Act 3 — 2000+ (modern train era)

```
Looking at: a newer train (red/blue AC train or white high-speed) below
Background: modern city skyline — high-rises, trees, clean streets
```

- Same low-angle ground-level perspective
- Newer train model below
- Background shows the new era's city atmosphere

### Optional Act 4 — High-speed era

Repeat the pass-through pattern via another window to reach the high-speed era.

## Visual flow summary

```
🚪 Pass through coal door (1902)
    ↓
🚃 Green train + old city (1970)
    ↓ zoom to girl → shift focus → 
🪟 Pass through girl's window
    ↓
🚄 New train + new city (2000)
    ↓
🪟 (optional) Pass through again
    ↓
🚄 High-speed train + modern city
```

## Key affordances

- **1902 coal wagons have no passenger windows** — use open side-doors as the transition portal
- **1970+ passenger trains have windows** — use windows as the transition portal
- **Door/window frame fills the screen** at the transition moment — this is the "portal" effect
- **Hard cuts and color fades are NOT needed** — the pass-through is the transition device
- **No text in images** — text is added manually later during assembly
