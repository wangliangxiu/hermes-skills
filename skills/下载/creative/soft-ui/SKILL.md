# Protocol: Soft UI Design

## Philosophy
Soft UI is warm, welcoming, and approachable. It uses gentle curves, muted colors, and subtle depth to create interfaces that feel safe and friendly. Think consumer apps, children's products, and wellness brands.

## 1. Core Rules

### 1.1 Typography
- Rounded or humanist typefaces: DM Sans, Plus Jakarta Sans, Nunito, Figtree.
- No sharp geometric fonts (Inter, Roboto).
- Generous line-height: 1.6-1.8 for body text.
- Headings: medium weight (500-600), not bold.

### 1.2 Color
- Warm base: off-white (#faf8f5), warm gray (#f0ede8).
- Accent: muted pastels — soft peach, sage green, lavender, dusty blue.
- Text: not pure black. Warm dark (#3a3530).
- High contrast is not the goal. Comfortable readability is.

### 1.3 Layout
- Generous whitespace. Everything breathes.
- Rounded corners: 12-16px for cards, 8-12px for buttons.
- Soft shadows: diffuse, low opacity, warm tint.
- Cards with subtle background tint, not white-on-white.

### 1.4 Components
- Buttons: filled with rounded shape (border-radius: 999px optional).
- Inputs: rounded, inner shadow subtle, gentle focus ring.
- Cards: tinted bg, soft shadow, rounded.
- Icons: filled (not outlined), rounded stroke caps.

## 2. Motion
- Everything fades and slides gently.
- Duration: 250-400ms. Easing: ease-out.
- Hover: slight lift (y: -2px) + shadow deepen.
- No aggressive animations. No parallax.

## 3. When to Use
- Consumer apps
- Wellness / health products
- Children's products
- Community platforms
- Any brand that wants to feel warm and safe
