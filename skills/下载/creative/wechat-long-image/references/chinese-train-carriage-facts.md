## Era 1: 1902 Steam-Era Coal Wagon (蒸汽运煤车)

- **Appearance:** Like a black shipping container — rectangular iron box body, riveted corrugated steel plates. No passenger windows at all.
- **Top:** **Completely open** — coal is loaded from above (敞顶). The user described it as "black container with open top."
- **Side doors:** Large double cargo doors that open fully to reveal the coal pile inside. "侧面大对开门" — they swing open to the sides.
- **Color:** Black iron, rusted black.
- **Usage:** Freight only, carries coal from mines. Not a passenger carriage.

**User's exact description:** "黑色集装箱一样的，顶部敞开装煤，侧面大开门"

## Era 2: 1970 Green Train (绿皮车)

- **Doors:** **Sliding doors** (推拉门) — they slide open along the OUTSIDE of the carriage on rails, not swinging inward. This saves interior space. "推拉门附着在车厢外侧不会占用太大地方"
- **Windows:** **Up-and-down sliding windows** (上下拉动式) — you push them UP to open.
- **Window security:** Every window has a **solid iron bar** fixed horizontally across the middle (实心铁棍中间横着). This was added to prevent theft/robbery (列车盗窃治安问题). "当时列车盗窃等治安问题，所以都在列车车窗加这个，防止列车打开很多"
- **Body color:** Green paint.
- **Note:** This is a passenger carriage, unlike Era 1.

## Era 3: 2000 Air-Conditioned Train (空调列车)

- **Doors:** **Aircraft-style airtight doors** (飞机一样的门) — flush with the body, not sliding on the outside.
- **Windows:** **Completely sealed** (全封闭不能开) — cannot be opened.
- **Body color:** White/blue paint, smooth streamlined body.

**User's exact words:** "空调车是和飞机一样的门" "空调高铁都不能开" (meaning the windows).

## Era 4: High-Speed Train (高铁)

- **Doors:** Same as air-conditioned train — aircraft-style airtight doors.
- **Windows:** Completely sealed — cannot be opened.
- **Body:** Streamlined white bullet train body.
